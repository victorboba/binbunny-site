Photograph it.
It's named and filed in a box.

BinBunny turns a photo into an inventory entry: what it is, how many, which box, what it's worth. Add one item at a time, bulk-add ten photos, photograph a whole pile, or film a room and get a list. Tag every container with a QR label, a barcode, or an NFC sticker and find anything in seconds.

Get BinBunny for iPhone

Free forever on your phone, PDF export included. Pro is 7 days free, or pay once for Lifetime.

Snap

Four ways to add: one item per photo, bulk add up to ten photos, a group shot where the AI finds every item and crops each its own photo, or a video walkthrough of a room. Type the names yourself, or let the AI name them with Pro.

File and tag

Boxes, bins, shelves, closets, rooms, garages, storage units, cars. Every location gets a QR label to print, or attach the barcode, QR code, or NFC tag already on the container.

Find

Search by name, category, notes, or location. Scan a label with the iPhone Camera, or tap an NFC tag, and the box opens in the app.

Report and value

The AI estimates what each item is worth; edit it any time. One tap makes a PDF of everything you own, with photos and totals, by location. Free, always: it's your record.

Share

One inventory for the whole household, up to four people. Everyone sees the same boxes and photos, and everyone can add. Members join with a code, no purchase needed.

Yours

Your inventory lives on your phone and exports to PDF or CSV whenever you like. Pro and Lifetime back it up to your account and sync it across devices.
